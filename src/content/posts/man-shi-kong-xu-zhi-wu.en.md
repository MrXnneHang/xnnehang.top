## Parallel timelines?

The story’s timeline is somewhat confusing, but a clever narrative weaves it all together. I could not tell whether I was trying to solve a mystery as I read or simply following along; it somewhat forced me to think.

Overall, it was good. _Dreams Come True_, its companion piece, and _Mother_ all resonated with me. Taken as a whole, however, its themes feel somewhat scattered, and what it has to say about death and lifespan feels faint—or rather, quite shallow.

That is no exaggeration. The novel and manga versions of _Three Days of Happiness_, [[After “A Year for Ten Thousand Yen”]], [[We Who Have No Tomorrow Fell in Love Yesterday]], [[Existentialist Reading Notes]], and [[I Come Alone, I Leave Alone]] have together given my thoughts about death and lifespan something like the effect of thematic reading. By comparison, this work’s discussion remains on the surface for me. Perhaps manga as a medium lacks some of the complexity that prose can provide.

Still, as the first book I have finished in one sitting in quite a while, it was a good start.
