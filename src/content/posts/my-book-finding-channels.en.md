## Z-Library

https://zh.1lib.sk/

![Z-Library interface](/screenshots/zlibrary.jpg)

Most published books that are not too obscure can be found here, usually as EPUB, PDF, or MOBI files. I often use it to look for light novels, literature, reference books, and finished web novels, then read them on my Boox Leaf 2. As a result, I have bought very few books since college; I only buy physical copies of books I think I will reread and want to keep.

## Anna's Archive

https://annas-archive.gl/

![Anna's Archive interface](/screenshots/安娜的档案.jpg)

Its domain seems to change often, so if it is unavailable, search Google for Anna's Archive. If you still cannot find it, it is probably keeping a low profile for a while.

Its selection and file formats are much the same as Z-Library's, though it also seems to include a great deal of academic literature. I have not had much use for that myself. If a book is not on Z-Library, try here.

## Kox.moe

https://kzo.moe/

![Kox.moe interface](/screenshots/漫画_kox_moe.jpg)

A site dedicated entirely to manga.

It appears to redirect between domains and requires registration and sign-in. It supports sending books to Kindle and offers EPUB and MOBI formats, but it does not support reading online.
