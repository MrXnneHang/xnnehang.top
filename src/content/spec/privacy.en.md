This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand page views, visitor counts, and aggregate engagement time so I can improve the content and site experience.

## Data collected and published

GA4 may collect access events such as page paths, page titles, and device and browser types, which Google processes. Any public statistics page on this site will show aggregate data only. It will not disclose IP addresses, individual visitors' browsing records, or other personally identifiable information.

“Reading time” refers to the user engagement time recorded by GA4. It is useful only as an overall trend and does not mean that a visitor was actively reading the entire time. Visits may not be recorded when someone uses an ad or tracking blocker, disables JavaScript, or has a browser that restricts analytics requests.

## Data coverage

Statistics begin accumulating only after GA4 tracking was officially enabled; complete historical traffic from before that point cannot be reconstructed. Public statistics are updated on a schedule and may therefore lag behind the latest data in GA4.

If you prefer not to participate in analytics, you can use your browser's tracking protection, install an analytics-blocking extension, or disable JavaScript. To learn how Google processes Analytics data, see [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
